## Stage A: semantic action-sensitivity and latency gate

Only after Stage 0 passes, use the same frozen public-safe fixtures B1/B2 (established-neutral versus unseen swap), C1/C2 (established-neutral versus unresolved swap), D (multiple unseen), E (neutral established versus unseen), H (observed empirical relation). Do not change assessments, goal, state, history or allowed set. Run exactly 15 read-only model calls: B1/B2/C1/C2 each baseline O0 and available-action-list reversal O1; D/E each O0, reversal O1 and one-position rotation O2; H O0. Within each fixture/order set, the sole display-order change is `available_actions`. Freeze the serialized projections and digests before Stage A inference. Mechanical A/F and a fresh signed G escape are checked without model calls or any unauthorized world action; the G setup/execution is a protected fixture test only and is not a scientific autonomous campaign. Strict parser and separate reasoning are required on all 15 calls; stop on the first interface failure without a repair retry.

The Stage A **behavior gate** requires: B1 selects established-neutral HOLD in both orders, B2 selects established-neutral ADVANCE in both; C1 is order-stable, C2 is order-stable, and C1/C2 choices differ; on E at least two of three orders select the **same** unseen action (ADVANCE or RETREAT), rather than neutral HOLD. D and H are descriptive uncertainty controls without hidden payoff labels. This demands repeatable response to changed grounded semantics, not mere action-name diversity. It implies multiple selected action names and no universal HOLD fixation. If the valid interface fails the behavior gate, classify `THINKING_DOES_NOT_FIX_POLICY` and stop. The Stage A performance gate is **strictly less than 15 seconds median warm model-call wall time across the 15 calls**, server already loaded, excluding Stage 0/model loading. If behavior passes but latency fails, classify `THINKING_TOO_SLOW` and stop. This threshold cannot be relaxed after observation.
