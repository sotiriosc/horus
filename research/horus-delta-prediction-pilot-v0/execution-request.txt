# New hypothesis

The previous studies may have placed Triangulation too early.

We asked the model to use Time / Relation / Direction while deciding what experiment to run.

A different possibility is:

1. first let reality provide observations;
2. compare observations;
3. identify a controlled delta;
4. infer a provisional relation from that delta;
5. use that relation to predict an unobserved case;
6. choose an experiment specifically because its outcome can support or contradict that prediction.

So the proposed loop is:

authenticated observations
        ↓
compare
        ↓
observed delta
        ↓
provisional relation
        ↓
prediction about unknown
        ↓
discriminating experiment
        ↓
reality
        ↓
support / contradiction
        ↓
repeat

This pilot tests whether that organization improves inquiry.

It does NOT assume the hypothesis is correct.

Interpretation:

Pattern 4:

Interpretation:
Potentially useful falsification behavior; quantify it, do not romanticize it.

Pattern 5:

Interpretation:
This placement hypothesis is unsupported.
