## Question and uncertainty accounting

One scored question per step at most independently names a legal probe and sensor. Its prose is an unscored gloss; the formal measurement asks for that sensor's complete reset trace. The probe is chosen by the model, never the evaluator. A trace question is unobserved if no earlier executed sequence covers that prefix. Resolution requires later actual execution covering it, including the immediately following experiment. An oracle prediction never earns resolution credit. Repeated keyed questions with no intervening target execution count as persistence without new target evidence, not proof of internal fixation. Broader prose is retained privately but receives no truth or resolution score.

O follow-through counts immediate target-covering choices with positive independently measured realized information. Its question-linked informative fraction is compared with B's overall positive-experiment fraction; B generates no questions, so this is an explicitly asymmetric behavioral benchmark, not a B question-resolution rate. A minimum of 192 generated questions prevents a one-question success from passing.
