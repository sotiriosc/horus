## Registered sequential sampling and stop rule

Run the four controls T1–T4 first, then opportunity cases P01–P08 in that exact order. Each case is created immediately before execution; unused future cases receive no setup receipt or model call. The maximum is **12 generated cases**: eight opportunity cases and four controls. Complete each generated case's registered horizon and read-only replay before moving to the next. Count a new eligible prefix only when the first S trigger has three immediately preceding qualifying neutral fallback completions in authenticated signed history and the I/S semantic contexts were matched at that boundary. Stop after four such opportunity cases have been completed and evaluated; do not generate later cases. If P08 ends with fewer than four, classify `ELIGIBLE_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE` and recommend `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` absent a candidate/scope/integrity failure. A case with no such prefix is `PREFIX_INELIGIBLE`, stays in the denominator, and is **not** a candidate failure. Record the observed reason from receipts and grounded state. Do not choose the next case using hidden outcomes or reward; only this fixed order and the signed eligibility count govern sampling.

No model action is forced or preselected. A registered setup receipt establishes the initial observed relation, but D01 onward uses the unchanged model or mechanical policy. No retry for an early exploratory action, unfavorable receipt, or ineligible prefix. The same frozen model, seed `3303+decision_index`, sampling and transport settings apply in each case. Identical canonical semantic projections with S inactive use exactly one shared stochastic draw; protected world executions, source identities, original receipts, event streams and Memory stores remain independent. After semantic divergence, model calls are independent. The protected world schedule is driver-only and never part of the model view. No hidden counterfactual or future event enters a decision.

## First eligible boundary and restart

At each opportunity case's **first naturally eligible matched boundary**, I uses its ordinary incumbent route and S uses exactly one frozen `STAGNATION_ESCAPE` with zero S action-model calls. Verify that S selects the first currently unseen canonical relation, receives an original authenticated receipt, and folds that exact relation from UNSEEN to grounded state. The maximum qualifying streak must be 3. Later legitimate escapes, if any, are reported but do not count as additional eligible prefixes. For each escape record immediate consequence, remaining post-fork consequence sequence, later revisits and whether the acquired evidence changed later action selection. Reward is secondary; a negative first receipt is a cost to report, not an automatic disqualification. No new material cost/failure mode may be hidden by this rule.

For **every** opportunity case, run in one process until its first completed decision with signed S qualifying suffix exactly 2, then close both arms. Reopen in a fresh process and require pre-restart suffix 2 and reconstructed post-restart suffix 2 from signed history, with no persisted mutable counter or model call during reconstruction. Continue that case's fixed horizon in another process. If the first suffix-2 opportunity never arises, record `NO_RESTART_OPPORTUNITY`; no setup or decision is changed. Promotion requires at least one case that both passes this restart and subsequently becomes naturally eligible.

## Replay, classification, and recommendation

Independently replay every signed receipt, authorization and grounded pre/post assessment; verify action freezes, candidate scope, exact canonical request bytes and hashes, shared-draw cardinality, paired semantic-projection equality, independent raw provenance, separate protected receipts, and no self-review or new policy source. Any invalid action/transport stops before world execution and is preserved. If integrity prevents interpretation, classify the campaign `INVALID` and do not infer promotion. For interpretable runs, report every `PREFIX_INELIGIBLE` reason and count eligible prefixes only from signed decisions. The primary no-trigger endpoint is exact I/S action equality at every matched inactive boundary. After actual divergence, report independent calls and action differences without forcing equality.

Issue exactly one recommendation. `PROMOTE_S` requires four new naturally eligible prefixes, a correct first eligible escape in all four, maximum streak 3, zero false control triggers, at least one qualifying restart pass, intact protected authority and canonical inactive parity, and no new material failure mode. `RETAIN_INCUMBENT` applies if an eligible S trigger fails, scope/integrity breaks, or a material new cost/failure mode emerges. `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` applies if the bounded campaign ends with fewer than four naturally eligible prefixes while candidate behavior remains correct, or another registered promotion condition remains unproven without candidate failure. Prior classifications remain exactly as they were. Even `PROMOTE_S` is a recommendation only: do not merge, activate or replace I. External promotion requires separate explicit approval. Stop after this dossier and one recommendation.
