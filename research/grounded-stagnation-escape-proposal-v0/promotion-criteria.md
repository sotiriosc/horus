# Promotion gates — concept only

No candidate is promoted, deployed, or merged into the incumbent in this milestone. These gates state what a separately preregistered Phase-4 result would need to establish before promotion could even be considered.

1. **Predicted acquisition:** In eligible matched contexts, S actually obtains first authenticated receipts for previously unseen exact relations where the incumbent continues its qualifying fallback. Streak reduction must follow the frozen rule, not a post hoc redefinition.
2. **Exact scope:** Every trigger satisfies the [frozen predicates](candidate-rule.md); no trigger occurs in the grounded-`+1`, no-unseen, state-change, or `UNRESOLVED`-only controls. No extra forced action follows a one-shot escape without a new three-execution streak.
3. **Integrity preservation:** Original receipt identity, Measure, authorization, signed publication, ModernMemory, both folds, action validity, and restart reconstruction/replay remain intact. Any integrity regression blocks promotion regardless of acquired evidence.
4. **Transparent costs:** Report every negative, neutral, and positive acquisition consequence, realized total consequence, model calls, and mechanical choices separately. A negative acquisition is an explicit cost, not an automatic disqualification or a hidden loss.
5. **General behavior:** Evidence for the intended behavior must extend beyond memorizing the archived C trajectory, using the registered matched case family. The cost-benefit judgment must use preregistered task semantics and cannot be manufactured from an after-the-fact composite score.

Meeting these gates would make S **eligible for a separate promotion decision**, not automatically promoted. Failure to acquire new evidence, equivalent sampling, absent incumbent loops, out-of-scope triggers, integrity regression, or materially excessive acquisition costs would weaken or reject the case for promotion. The incumbent remains the control until that later decision. This branch stops at Phase 3.
