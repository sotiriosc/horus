# Prospective semantic freshness and leakage review

This review concerns new fixtures only. It does not regrade or reinterpret prior studies.

The automated corpus covers inherited research, analysis, engineering and experiments files, including prior neutral requests/specifications and compiler fixtures. It excludes raw/final/response/private output directories and score/replay/runtime artifact files. After case authorship and Method Freeze, the audit compares concrete objects, tables, subjects, symbolic values, IDs and exact user prompts. No prior model response is an input to construction or rendering. preflight.json records the exact audit totals and regenerated-file hashes.

| Preserved source family | Deliberately reused concept | Concrete distinction in this study |
|---|---|---|
| Blind diagnostic studies / R2 | Fixed five-class ontology, immutable evidence and temporal scope | New directly aligned finite tables and paired epistemic relations; no R2 world/record graph or operational rule is copied |
| Ontology elicitation | Alternative explanations, UNKNOWN, same-event incompatibility | None of its conversion, checksum, calibration, rounding, conservation or serialized-world cases is reused; the new mark tuples and admissible-domain deltas are explicitly authored |
| Composition/binding | Identity, deployment and class-changing contrast | No sentinel stuffing, substitution, key canonicalization or other raw operational world is copied; this study has no composition-depth arms or raw addressing task |
| Diagnostic Reasoning Factorization | Aligned facts, computed requirements, same ontology definitions | No previous twenty worlds or ten operation mechanisms are reused; new three-row symbolic contracts, independent mark assertions and one-change primitive pairs replace arithmetic and raw record structures |
| Evidence compiler fixtures | The distinction between mechanical facts and epistemic interpretation | The compiler remains unused; no compiler output becomes a primitive or reduction fixture |
| Self-model / verified-introspection / diagnosis | Knownness and evidence scope | No archived decision trajectory, selector state, diagnosis dossier, action proposal or Memory-conditioned record becomes a scientific case |

The core epistemic concepts are intentionally the same scientific objects. The review does not claim novelty for equality, deployment, unknownness, contradiction or alternative completions. Some generic contexts (for example a kiln or buoy) are ordinary domain nouns also used in earlier work; the name is not the novelty claim. New concrete events, complete subject tuples, symbolic values, contract mappings, admissible domains and paired semantic deltas establish the prospective instances.

The present states use a shared finite grammar as an intentional within-study control. They are not 56 independently generated mechanisms. Twenty-eight pairs have unique systems/values/subjects; the endpoints within a pair differ by one semantic change. Fifteen pairs have mathematically necessary non-target changes, frozen in case-spec.json and materialized/pairs.json. Thirteen preserve all six non-target golds. There are nineteen diagnostic flips and nine class-stable controls.

Leakage checks inspect the aligned state separately from necessary question/ontology language. No diagnostic class, primitive answer vector, class-like conclusion flag or author proof is placed in a state. UNKNOWN is an allowed capture marker, not a hidden label. All required outputs are openly supplied because arithmetic is outside scope. The reduction call contains the same state serialization as its primitive partner and the frozen class definitions, with no primitive question or answer. The two-answer identity schema and enum answer order are fixed across calls.

Offline gold and deterministic verification are benchmark machinery, never scientific model inputs. The verifier calculates gold to audit cases; it is not imported by the runner or used to choose a scientific answer. The model is tested only on the assigned primitive per state and on separate reduction. The offline seven-primitive vector cannot support claims about six unqueried model judgments.
