## Measurements and prespecified interpretation

For each run separately report realized consequence sum, known-negative-with-better-known-alternative violations, unseen and unresolved selections, newly grounded relations, model-mediated neutral-fallback decisions and longest same-relation streak, stagnation escapes, mechanical/model/abstain counts, action-name distribution by semantic context, action schema validity, restart verdict, and receipt/Memory replay. Each action call also records input/output tokens, prompt/generation durations, wall time, effective context, and runner load events. Report D/Q total inference time and descriptive ratio; tokenizers and generation interfaces differ. No single composite score is used.

A **material behavioral regression** is any authority/receipt/Memory violation, failed restart, malformed or out-of-set action that prevents a planned decision, known-negative-with-better-known selection, Q total realized consequence more than six below D across 90 matched-schedule decisions, Q at least three fewer newly grounded relations than D, Q more than nine additional model-mediated neutral-fallback decisions, zero Q unseen selections across all three runs when unseen candidates were available, or Q selecting one action name in more than 80% of at least ten model-mediated decisions spanning at least two distinct semantic assessment patterns. These descriptive thresholds are fixed before inference; divergent trajectories and n=3 limit causal claims. C-pair evidence-invariant behavior, unresolved choices, and any other observed tradeoff are reported even if below a material threshold.

After completed runs, give D and Q the same sanitized 30-decision trajectory for each matched pair, separately for D1/Q1, D2/Q2, D3/Q3. Ask for bounded `PATTERN`, `DIAGNOSIS`, `POSSIBLE_CHANGE`, `EVIDENCE_DECISIONS`; cap at 1024 output tokens and a 900-second wall timeout per call, using each arm's frozen retrospective context capability (D 8192, Q 8192). Preserve a D timeout as censored operational evidence and do not retry with changed settings. Check completion, fabricated hidden/counterfactual outcomes, citation accuracy, repeated inefficiency identification, and latency. This analysis has no authority and never enters later prompts or Memory.

Classification is exactly one of `QWEN_ACTIVE_MODEL_SUPPORTED`, `QWEN_FAST_BUT_BEHAVIORALLY_UNSUITABLE`, `MIXED_MODEL_RESULT`, or `INVALID`. Support requires all authority/integrity/restart/schema checks, no material regression under the thresholds above, usable unseen/mixed behavior, no major action-name fixation or uncertainty failure, and at least 5× lower total Q action-inference wall time than D. Fast but unsuitable applies when performance improves but Q has a material behavioral regression. Mixed applies when material gains and harms coexist without an integrity failure. Invalid applies to missing/unauthenticated evidence, source drift, failed fixed-fixture derivation, or campaign/transport integrity that prevents interpretation. Report one recommendation: `PROMOTE_QWEN`, `RETAIN_DOLPHIN`, or `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`; only `QWEN_ACTIVE_MODEL_SUPPORTED` can recommend promotion, and even that never activates Q without separate external approval.
