## 36. Limitations

One pinned model, two opaque families, twelve matched seeds per family, one stationary-versus-changed consequence relation, two episodes and at most seven retained events per context. Seeds repeat across families and stages; these are matched observations, not independent population samples. Chronology and authentic consequences are presented together; no internal reasoning mechanism is isolated. P1 is descriptive and latency conditional on old P0. No model-controlled environment interaction, long-horizon adaptation, indefinite Memory, weight/chat learning or general drift detector is tested. Trusted external emitter, registry, audit and simulator reset remain the authority boundary.

## 37. Narrowest defensible conclusion

Both opaque families met every frozen criterion: authenticated new episode-2 experience changed stateless Map proposals relative to matched consistent CONTROL while the older authentic observations remained fully present. This is input-conditioned proposal behavior, not persistent model learning, internal belief revision, causal world-model learning, weight learning, RL, AGI or RSI.
