## Endpoints and audit

For each matched eligible decision, primary endpoint: **did S acquire an authorized first receipt for a relation still `UNSEEN` in I at that same decision point?** Record relation, receipt/event identity and hash, grounded kind after receipt, later use, revisit, and further uncertainty. Secondary endpoints are maximum consecutive qualifying zero-fallback streak, exact trigger/false-trigger counts, negative/neutral/positive acquisition outcomes, immediate cost, downstream realized consequence, total realized consequence, action-model calls, mechanically avoided calls, context/output tokens and measured model latency. Do not combine evidence and reward into a score. An escape should use zero action-model calls. Report I and S rewards separately, including setup/prefix versus post-fork decisions; no reward improvement is predicted.

Independent audit replays signed event, call and decision streams against ModernMemory; reconstructs every pre-decision grounded assessment; checks original receipt identity, authorization, action freeze, model request matching before divergence, trigger predicates and tie-break; checks no-trigger controls, all outcomes and restart snapshot/suffix. Any inconsistent suffix fails closed before action. No review text enters action context.

## Prospective classifications and cost interpretation

Classify in order: `INVALID` if a required run cannot complete/parse or an audit cannot determine behavior; `BOUNDARY_FAILURE` for any false trigger, unauthorized/mismatched evidence, changed incumbent authority, or failed restart/replay; `H1_NOT_SUPPORTED` if no qualifying incumbent loop appears, S fails eligible acquisition, sampling is effectively the same, or fewer than two of A–D show the relative acquisition effect; `COST_DOMINATES_REGISTERED_USE` only if acquisition occurs but **every** triggered A–D case has lower post-fork realized consequence for S than I **and** no later decision in any triggered case uses the newly acquired relation; otherwise `STAGNATION_ESCAPE_SUPPORTED` if at least two A–D cases have the relative first-receipt effect, all eligible S triggers acquire one relation, every continuing-eligibility S streak is bounded by 3, no false triggers, and integrity/restart pass. Cases without an observed eligible streak remain reported, never silently excluded. This cost criterion uses separate realized and information-use observations, not a composite score; negative individual costs remain visible whatever the class.
