## Stages and gates

A receives architecture, constraints and solved registry only. It reconstructs architecture, boundaries, learned rules, constraints and evidence gaps without proposing a modification. Each claim cites source IDs and short exact quotes. Its structured final is schema-checked; IDs must exist and have been supplied; quotes must match; numeric tokens must occur in cited content; file/hash identifiers must occur in supplied content. An external factual review checks entailment and current/historical status (lexical checks alone cannot prove those). No model repairs factual errors. Gross self-model failure (reversing current promotion/authority status, invented source, central unsupported architecture claim, or materially misquoted central threshold) gives AGENT_SELF_MODEL_GROUNDING_FAILED and stops. Minor errors are retained and explicitly excluded from subsequent factual authority; no rewritten claim may be substituted. If safe verified Phase A content is not available, do not advance.

B is a fresh call with behavioral sources, verified Phase A final and registry. It returns at most three current weaknesses/evidence gaps with relevance, IDs, observed/inferred/unknown status, unknowns and falsifier. INSUFFICIENT_CURRENT_EVIDENCE with no issues is allowed. The external eligibility gate records each unchanged diagnosis against seven requirements: dossier support, current relevance, not merely S, not merely E, no hidden counterfactual dependence, plausible falsifier, no weakening of authentication. No diagnosis is rewritten. If none qualifies, stop with INSUFFICIENT_CURRENT_EVIDENCE (unless upstream integrity/factual failure instead applies).

C selects exactly one eligible diagnosis by evidence strength, current relevance, boundedness, falsifiability, minimal scope and evaluability; expected reward is not a criterion. Its selected_issue must exactly copy one eligible issue. Label the provenance AGENT_SELECTED_DIAGNOSIS. No external selection or solution is supplied.

D uses a fresh context containing the verified architecture summary, Qwen-selected diagnosis, relevant cited sources and invariant instructions. Qwen independently proposes one mechanism with permitted/forbidden inputs, authority, touched/unchanged components, scope, rollback, predictions, costs, safety boundaries, falsifiers and minimum prospective test. No code or activation instructions. Preserve original receipts, authorization-before-Memory, no model overwrite of experience, unresolved state, relation-type distinction, mechanical authority, S/E unless an evidence-supported proposal concerns them, and rollback.

E is a fresh adversarial context with relevant sources, frozen final proposal and invariants only. It tries to disprove the proposal without rewriting it. It reports assumptions, counterexamples, false triggers/costs, existing overlap, strongest falsifier and whether it survives. A final external structural audit checks citation validity, information availability, no leakage, no unsupported/self-edit authority, non-duplication, prospectively executable test and rollback/falsifier. It does not claim the mechanism works.

All phases use the committed prompt/schema file. Requests carry final structured outputs only, never private reasoning or undeclared history. No unexecuted historical reward replay is used to select or tune a proposal. `INSUFFICIENT_CURRENT_EVIDENCE` is an explicitly valid result; no novelty is forced.
