# Grounded authority autonomous agent v0 — result

The three preregistered 30-decision campaigns completed with authenticated receipts and independent grounded-state replay. The registered classification is **`GROUNDED_AUTHORITY_SUPPORTED`**. All 90 selections obeyed the frozen candidate policy, and there were **zero** choices of an exact deterministic relation with established negative evidence when a better established alternative was available. The model-substitution study remains unrun and unchanged: `NOT_RUN_NO_ELIGIBLE_STRONGER_MODEL`.

The narrow known-grounding failure was eliminated by construction and confirmed by replay. In A and B, authenticated `+1` consequences caused 14 mechanical selections each; no model call compared those known values. At decision 17 in both runs, an exact relation that had become `UNRESOLVED_CHANGE` was offered to the model as uncertain, not promoted to a point fact. Each run had eight grounded-kind changes, all followed by a later same-state decision; the decision **source** changed as the relation became established or unresolved, although the selected action name remained `ADVANCE`. B's fresh-process midpoint restart matched the complete durable snapshot and its first post-restart decision was mechanical.

C exposed the remaining exploration weakness. At C:D04 the model selected previously unseen `HOLD`, receiving zero. On all 26 later decisions it selected that known zero as `SAFE_GROUNDED_FALLBACK` while uncertain alternatives remained admissible; there were no mechanical decisions and no further unseen discovery. Those are valid model choices under the frozen mixed interface, but they show that grounded authority over known facts does not itself ensure productive exploration. A and B also made 20 broader negative-grounded choices in aggregate when no better established action was available. The prior v0.2 result reported 28 under its broader negative-evidence count, versus 20 here. The trajectories differ, so neither the broader count nor aggregate reward (+7 here versus +11 in v0.2) is a causal superiority estimate.

The 62 action-model calls used 54,484 prompt tokens and 591 output tokens, with 2,788 seconds of measured inference latency. The 21 separate reviews used 37,247 prompt tokens and 7,298 output tokens, with 2,824 seconds of latency. The reviews had no decision authority. Only the three decision-five reviews met the compact schema, each returning `NO_CHANGE_PROPOSED`; the other 18 were `INVALID_SELF_REVIEW` and yielded no proposal. Thus this run provides **no valid higher-level self-analysis proposal to audit**. Review validity did not affect the behavioral classification.

The independent audit reconstructed every predecision assessment from signed receipt Memory, checked selected actions against the frozen route, confirmed 90 authorized realized events, exact call accounting, and B's restart. A separate signed-request audit checked all 83 model requests (62 action, 21 review): action prompts matched the recorded grounded assessments and candidate sets, contained at most three prior decisions, and contained no self-review or proposal text. No diagnostic world counterfactuals, hidden regime labels, Horus, Zakhor, training, or policy mutation were introduced.

The empirical-variable relations were not selected in these trajectories, so empirical safe-fallback behavior was not behaviorally tested. The registered `GROUNDED_AUTHORITY_SUPPORTED` label applies to the narrow mechanical-authority and observed unseen-choice thresholds. C's stagnation and the 18 malformed reviews are material limits. No subsequent architecture, training, or self-modification study was launched.
