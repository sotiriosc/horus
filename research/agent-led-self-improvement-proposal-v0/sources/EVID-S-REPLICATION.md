# Grounded stagnation escape v0 — completed replication and promotion-readiness study

**Campaign status: `VALID`. Registered classification: `COST_OR_SCOPE_BLOCKS_PROMOTION`. Promotion recommendation: `RETAIN_INCUMBENT`.** The frozen S rule acquired a first authenticated receipt at the matched decision-4 boundary in all six eligible cases while the unchanged I arm left that exact relation `UNSEEN`. The effect appeared in states 1, 2 and 3, with both `ADVANCE` and `HOLD` as repeated fallback actions and six distinct acquired relation identities over the full horizons. The conclusion is limited to this prospectively registered case family, whose shared prefixes and scheduled worlds are not independent population samples.

There were 10 S escape receipts across the six cases: one negative, six neutral and three positive. Every escape used the first currently unseen canonical action at the exact reconstructed threshold of 3 and made no action-model call at that boundary. The first six D04 receipts satisfy the registered relative-acquisition endpoint. R4 shows actual later grounded use; most other newly acquired relations were not revisited within their horizons. R6 demonstrates that I can naturally encounter new evidence later and that S's measured first-new-relation lead here was one decision. Reward totals describe realized paths, not a general safety or value guarantee for exploration.

The six no-trigger controls had **zero S escapes**. C1 respected the established +1 mechanical ceiling; C2 had no unseen alternative; C3 kept the conflicting `HOLD` relation unresolved; C4's repeated empirical `HOLD` observations never qualified as a deterministic established-zero fallback; and C6 reset the relation-specific streak after its state change. I/S actions matched in C1–C4 and C6. **C5 failed action parity without a trigger:** after the same setup and state-changing D01 receipt, both arms sent the same D02 model request (SHA-256 `0e5f079446e11a16bf6ec025101ac3131253552ca7cf791b3e0164e627fc3112`), yet I's recorded output selected `HOLD` for 0 and S's selected `ADVANCE` for −1. Their raw-output hashes differ and are preserved in [verification](verification.json). The recorded request equality and absence of a candidate escape do not establish the cause of that model-output divergence. The preregistered conservative promotion gate treats divergent actions in a no-trigger control as a blocker; no repeat inference or post hoc gate change was made.

Restart B copied R4 S immediately after its D04 escape; a fresh process reconstructed suffix 0 and mechanically selected the new +1 `HOLD` at D05. All 14 matched/copy prefixes passed signed event, decision, Memory and model-call comparison. No review call occurred.
