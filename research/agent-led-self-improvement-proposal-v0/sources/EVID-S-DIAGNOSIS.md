# Completed-campaign diagnosis (Phase 2)

This table retains each campaign's registered interpretation. `INTEGRITY_FAILURE` means the evidence/authority path broke, `KNOWLEDGE_FAILURE` means relevant grounded outcomes were unavailable, and `DECISION_FAILURE` means available evidence was used poorly by the action route. `INTERFACE/OPERATIONS` separately marks invalid output or absent response: those do **not** prove a behavioral or integrity failure. A study can expose a limit without its own validity being in doubt. No invalid pilot inference is reused as behavioral evidence.

Across the valid completed campaigns, the protected receipt, authorization, durable Memory, and grounded replay paths held; no observed `INTEGRITY_FAILURE` is reported in these results. Unseen or indistinguishable futures are genuine `KNOWLEDGE_FAILURE`s. Invalid interfaces/timeouts remain invalid operational results and are not upgraded to behavioral evidence.
