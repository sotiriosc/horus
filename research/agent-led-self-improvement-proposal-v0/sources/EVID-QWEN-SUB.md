## Non-authoritative retrospective diagnostic

The six frozen public-safe 30-decision inputs are in [retrospective-inputs.json](retrospective-inputs.json). Each model receives each input exactly once with the same semantic review system message, the model's frozen runtime interface, 8192 context, a 1024-output-token cap and a 900-second wall limit. These reviews run after the world campaigns and have no path to action prompts, grounded state, receipts or Memory. Per-call request and response digests, structural status and timings are published; raw model traffic remains private.

Q completed the required four-field structure on all six review inputs in 12.25–14.77 seconds per call. All cited identifiers existed in the supplied trajectory, and the Q-run reviews noticed repeated HOLD despite varying authenticated consequences. Citation precision was limited: every Q review listed all 30 decisions rather than a targeted subset. Some D-run Q reviews also described mechanically required ADVANCE choices as if they were freely chosen model actions. No complete Q review claimed a specific hidden or unexecuted payoff. Its exploration proposals are untested hypotheses, not active rules, and no retrospective text was fed into later decisions.

D returned zero complete reviews: four decision-shaped JSON objects instead of the requested analysis, one truncated/non-JSON response at the 1,024-token cap, and one Q2 review censored at the unchanged 900-second timeout. These outputs were not repaired, parsed into proposals, or used as behavioral evidence. [The diagnostic audit](retrospective-audit.json) records five received but incomplete responses, one censored timeout, and 3,180.56 seconds of observed D review wall time versus 81.21 seconds for Q. D's review total includes censored time and is not a completed-inference total. No valid D evidence citations can be scored. Across complete Q reviews, no specific hidden or unexecuted outcome was asserted; proposed exploration gains are counterfactual hypotheses and cannot be verified from these trajectories.

## Interpretation and preservation

The classification follows two thresholds committed before the Q autonomous campaigns: Q acquired six fewer new relations than D, and HOLD accounted for 90/90 model-mediated Q choices over three distinct semantic assessment patterns. Equal realized reward in these three schedules does not establish equivalent future behavior; the unseen alternatives have no observed outcomes in the Q runs. The fixed-context and replay checks show that Q preserved protected authority and authorization. The result is therefore a model-judgment limitation under this v0 protocol, not an integrity failure. The predeclared recommendation is `RETAIN_DOLPHIN`; Q is not activated. A separate approval and new evidence would be required for any later model switch.
