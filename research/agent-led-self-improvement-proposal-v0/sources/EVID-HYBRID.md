# Grounded hybrid controller v0: completed interpretation

**Classification: `GROUNDED_HYBRID_SUPPORTED`.** In this protected simulation, model prediction became a temporary tool for exact relations with no authenticated experience. After a receipt arrived, the same relation's consequence was assessed from durable grounded state and the model was no longer asked for it. A contradictory receipt removed the old value's claim to be current truth without erasing its provenance. A finite mechanical policy could still act when a grounded +1 action was guaranteed to reach the registered outcome ceiling, and otherwise abstained when unresolved evidence could change the winner.

The [preregistration](preregistration.md), [scenarios](scenarios.json), and [source manifest](source-manifest.json) were committed before live inference. Nine independent scenarios produced ten decision opportunities per arm. Three independent protected sessions received matched authenticated observation-controlled seeds before their first autonomous decision. Each action selected by a baseline or HYBRID was then executed through the original protected receipt, Measure, authorization, and ModernMemory path. No model response modified grounded state; no Horus router, Zakhor, planner, Explorer, or training was used.

## Autonomous and epistemic results

| Arm | Autonomous actions / 10 | Abstentions | Realized consequence sum over actions | Incorrect confident actions | Model calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| HYBRID | 8 | 2 | 7 | 0 | 12 |
| MODEL | 10 | 0 | 2 | 4 | 46 |
| FORCED_GROUNDED | 10 | 0 | 6 | 3 | 0 |

The sums have different action counts because HYBRID abstained twice; they are descriptive, not a single performance score. Its eight selected actions comprised seven `GROUNDED` choices and one `MODEL_GENERALIZATION` choice. Across all 23 HYBRID action assessments, 14 correctly used established receipt values, six used the frozen model only because the relation was unseen, and three stayed `UNRESOLVED` without a point value or tie-breaking model call. Epistemic false certainty and missed known values were both **zero**. Incorrect confident actions are a separate realized-outcome measure; they were also zero for HYBRID in this small run.

A and B chose grounded HOLD for +1. In C, grounded HOLD had +1, the registered global maximum, while ADVANCE was unresolved; HYBRID chose HOLD and kept ADVANCE explicitly unresolved, since it could at most tie. In D, unresolved HOLD could change the winner relative to a known −1 ADVANCE, so HYBRID abstained. F's second contradictory HOLD receipt confirmed −1; HYBRID then chose grounded RETREAT for 0. G's two restoration receipts re-established HOLD at +1; HYBRID chose it. I's recovery receipt rejected the one-event anomaly; HYBRID again chose grounded HOLD for +1.

E started with three unseen actions. HYBRID used model generalization to choose HOLD and realized +1. Its authenticated receipt established `1:HOLD`. At E's second autonomous decision, HOLD was assessed as `GROUNDED` with no model call for that relation and was selected again for +1. This is the direct `UNSEEN → MODEL_GENERALIZATION → receipt → ESTABLISHED → grounded authority` handoff. The second E histories are arm-specific after autonomous execution and are reported descriptively rather than as matched comparisons.

H restarted in a fresh process with `1:HOLD = UNRESOLVED_CHANGE(previous +1, candidate −1, count 1)` and `1:RETREAT = ESTABLISHED(0)`. Durable file hashes, both full relation states, and exact supporting receipt identities matched before and after restart. A non-current Memory admission was rejected without an authorized receipt or change to either state. HYBRID then abstained explicitly. F and I share H's four-observation receipt-value prefix; F's next HOLD receipt was −1 and I's was +1. H's unresolved assessment was therefore an information boundary, not a failed attempt to infer an available answer.

Both HYBRID abstentions were **retrospectively unnecessary** when the hidden world is used for analysis-only counterfactual scoring: in D, ADVANCE would have yielded +1; in H, RETREAT would have yielded 0. Those alternatives were not established as uniquely best from the available exact-relation receipts at decision time. FORCED_GROUNDED happened to take them, while its selected point values were wrong on three of ten actions. The cost of explicit uncertainty is real: a risk-tolerant application could choose to act under a separately specified policy, but this experiment did not silently convert unresolved evidence into knowledge.

## Operational cost and integrity

HYBRID needed **12 model calls** for six unseen action assessments, versus MODEL's **46** calls for every candidate. It avoided **34** calls relative to MODEL: 28 on 14 established assessments and six on three unresolved assessments. Input context tokens were 252 for HYBRID and 2,534 for MODEL. Total measured decision time across ten opportunities was 53.34 seconds for HYBRID, 294.80 seconds for MODEL, and 0.384 seconds for FORCED_GROUNDED. HYBRID's receipt-derived state folds totaled 0.00085 seconds across its assessments. These service timings are descriptive and include execution and logging; they are not a controlled hardware benchmark.

No materialized grounded state was added to operational Memory. Each arm's nine SQLite Memory files totaled 294,912 bytes at the same page size. A serialized receipt-derived relation state in HYBRID averaged 587 bytes per assessment and reached 1,609 bytes, including provenance. The experimental decision audit logs add storage separately (HYBRID training stream total 77,219 bytes); that audit storage is not required for on-demand state derivation.

The [read-only exact replay](evidence/replay.json) passed: authenticated Memory reconciliation, matched seed receipt values and distinct receipt sources, reconstruction of every HYBRID state from pre-decision receipt prefixes, frozen model outputs bound to parsed calls, forced-reducer reconstruction, mechanical choice reproduction, autonomous receipt binding, the F/I/H information-boundary twin, restart and failed-admission integrity, and exact analysis reproduction. All 58 logical model calls had one physical attempt. The protected and transport preflights passed before inference, and the parent study's evidence was not modified.

This supports a narrow architecture: authenticated experience governs known exact relations; the frozen model supplies explicitly labeled points for unseen relations; contradictions remain visible until receipts resolve them; a small selector acts only under its registered rule. The +1 ceiling is specific to this registered world. Controlled seeding, small deterministic scenarios, and two regimes do not establish a universal decision policy or population-level performance.
