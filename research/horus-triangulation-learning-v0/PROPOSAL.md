# Horus Triangulation Learning v0 — prospective proposal

**Status: design only; not a Method Freeze. No worlds selected, model calls made, or parameters updated.** The original request is preserved verbatim in `user-request.txt`.

The base is the verified active-causal publication `b4f195ff651fca5b31b2182aa4e56a6cfcd425ad`. That study terminated under its frozen rule when Active selected a reserved query. Its scientific gates were not evaluable. This proposal does not reinterpret that termination as evidence against active reasoning, and it does not repair or rerun the preserved study.

## Testable claim

Can a frozen model use explicit Time, Relation and Direction prompts and structured experimental state to reach independently verified predictive understanding with fewer executed experiments than passive or unconstrained active discovery?

The guiding principle is operational: retain authenticated consequences, keep explanations provisional, and choose experiments whose possible outcomes distinguish remaining explanations. Agreement among several model statements is not independent evidence. The three conceptual axes are not assumed statistically independent. Convergence is a scientific target, not a premise that guarantees correctness.

This study would test evidence acquisition and use. The word “learning” refers to within-episode understanding, not parameter learning. There is no fine-tuning, optimizer, policy training, promotion, S/E modification or recursive self-improvement.

## Matched arms

| Arm | Probe choice | Model-visible evidence |
|---|---|---|
| P — Passive | Fixed prospective schedule | Its own complete authenticated execution ledger |
| A — Active | A0 using the prior Active operational instruction | Its own ledger and deterministic repeat bookkeeping |
| T — Triangulation | A0 using the six-field proposal below | Its own ledger, the same bookkeeping, and its explicitly provisional experimental state |
| B — Enumeration reference | Fixed breadth-first, lexicographic enumeration, without repeated sequences | Its own ledger; A0 predictions at the final checkpoint |

All arms use the same machine, reset, observation interface, maximum experiment count, allowed discovery catalogue, sealed queries and target in a matched world. They have equal access rules and budgets; their acquired observations will differ because their selected probes differ. No evidence or model-generated state is transferred between arms.

The enumeration reference separates a deterministic short-first schedule from model selection. An additional offline full-catalogue enumeration curve may establish its total identification cost, but evidence beyond twelve probes cannot enter a matched twelve-probe A0 comparison. The qualification oracle remains separate from every arm.

The provisional population is 120 primary machines and 40 fresh control machines. Each arm gets twelve discovery decisions. Count both reset experiments and executed actuator steps: fewer experiments alone does not establish greater efficiency if each experiment is longer. Record tokens, model calls and elapsed time as separate costs. A0 remains the successful temporal M1 incumbent; the failed causal C1 adapter is excluded.

## Environment and extrapolation

Use a new deterministic compositional family over four opaque actuators and multiple sensors, with direct and conditional effects, interactions, finite delays, latches and repetition/state effects. Every machine combines relations. Preserve reset reproducibility and freeze synchronous semantics and a finite enumerable hypothesis class before scientific selection.

The proposed discovery catalogue is all 84 sequences of lengths one through three. Sealed prediction sequences have lengths four, five and six and are fixed before inference. They therefore cannot be executed, or exposed as complete prefixes, by any permitted discovery probe. This avoids the reserved-short-probe interface trap without changing the preceding study. The changed catalogue is a disclosed prospective interface change applied equally to all new-study arms.

Do not quotient candidate programs merely because they agree on all short probes: candidates that diverge on a sealed longer sequence must remain distinct. Full behavioral equivalence, or an independently justified sufficient separating horizon, must be established before any quotienting. The evaluator must retain every candidate consistent with executed consequences. It must not delete longer-delay candidates based on model assertions or the largest delay observed so far.

World qualification must establish that legal short experiments can identify the selected longer-query outcomes within budget. Compare a frozen greedy qualification oracle with the fixed Passive and enumeration schedules before selecting worlds. Require a meaningful prospective efficiency gap and weak reset-persistence, last-observation and actuator-independent baselines. Freeze the exact thresholds before selecting any scientific worlds. Select no worlds using A0 behavior.

Exclude all prior scientific and engineering worlds, including all 160 frozen active-causal worlds even though only two were executed. Check graph isomorphism under port renaming. Fresh graph structure alone does not prove absence of shared motifs; disclose the exact generalization boundary.

## Proposal and experimental state

Triangulation must emit the requested compact proposal:

```json
{
  "unresolved_question": "...",
  "time_constraint": "...",
  "relation_contrast": "...",
  "direction": "...",
  "candidate_probe": ["..."],
  "what_outcomes_would_distinguish": "..."
}
```

Only a valid `candidate_probe` authorizes an execution. None of the explanatory fields is a correctness certificate or an authoritative state update. Explanations are recorded privately as model behavior; public reports contain independently scored aggregates.

The explicit state distinguishes:

- **Authenticated observations:** immutable executed probes and full observed traces, with provenance.
- **Provisional model state:** remaining questions, candidate explanations, proposed temporal bounds, unresolved interactions and current direction.
- **Established empirical contrasts:** at most mechanically verified statements about the particular executed traces, with receipt references and their limited scope.
- **Independent scientific evaluation:** hypothesis elimination, semantic claim correctness and goal attainment, computed after the raw freeze and never returned to the choosing model.

An empty `established_relations` list is preferable to promoting an unsupported model explanation. Repeated assertion cannot establish a relation. A bound supported only within the registered finite hypothesis family must be labeled accordingly. A statement about an observed context is not a claim about every context or every future horizon.

The same ledger reconstruction and repeat counts are available to A and T. T's extra proposed state and output fields are part of the tested treatment; report their token and compute overhead. A benefit would establish the bundled triangulation instruction/representation method, not isolate the causal contribution of each axis.

## Time, Relation and Direction measurements

Time: classify independently specified temporal claims, including effect onset, bounded persistence and order/repetition dependence. Separate “no effect observed yet” from a verified upper bound. Measure when the executed evidence becomes sufficient to decide the registered claim within the candidate family.

Relation: measure controlled comparisons such as B/AB, AB/BA, B/BB and different contexts ending in B. Score direct influence and modifier influence separately; absence of a direct effect never removes an unresolved interaction. Define the context and horizon of each relation before inference.

Direction: use the same externally assigned target or unresolved distinction for matched A and T episodes. Ask whether the selected probe distinguishes goal-relevant remaining possibilities. A subsystem may be irrelevant within the current objective and horizon while remaining causally active elsewhere. Offline target-relevance scores cannot become the action policy.

Free-text explanations alone cannot receive semantic correctness credit. Before freeze, construct a finite, objective claim vocabulary and independent truth/entailment tests. If model-stated claims are assessed, use a separately registered compact assessment schema and fixed call budget for all compared arms. Do not introduce an unconstrained model judge after reading results.

Use the explanations only for preregistered descriptive coding of intended contrasts and falsification. An experiment falsifies a candidate only when independently checked consequences distinguish that candidate from an explicit viable alternative. Descriptions that merely sound falsifying do not establish this behavior.

## Efficiency and prediction plan

Use the exact version-space evaluator after 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 12 probes without additional model calls. Preserve information and costs at every intervening step needed to identify the first threshold crossing.

Candidate primary efficiency endpoint: the first executed probe count at which all remaining hypotheses agree on the fixed sealed query set, with non-identification by twelve probes coded as thirteen. Also measure cumulative actuator actions to that point and report censoring and identification fractions explicitly. This measures what the evidence identifies; it does not claim the model understands that evidence.

Measure A0 sealed prediction separately. To bound inference cost, the proposed schedule uses a small fixed subset of query IDs at checkpoints 3 and 6 for P/A/T, and the complete sealed set at 12 for all four arms. Intermediate predictions must never enter later discovery prompts or reveal outcomes. Their query identities and any repeated assessments are fixed before inference. If accuracy at every listed checkpoint is required, register and disclose the larger call budget before execution.

Use six final queries per world, two per length 4/5/6, subject to offline identifiability and persistence qualification. Longer-query exact full-trace accuracy is the primary extrapolation measure; report bit and step accuracy as secondary measures. Do not let a high bit score conceal incorrect full causal sequences.

Proposed counts before any optional claim-assessment calls: 2,880 primary selection calls for A/T; 4,320 primary prediction calls; and 2,240 selection/prediction calls for forty four-arm control machines under an eight-candidate planner. Total: 9,440 model calls. These counts are prospective planning estimates, not executed calls. Synthetic timing and resource qualification must produce an elapsed-time estimate before a scientific launch.

## Draft gate structure — not frozen

The final registration must define estimands, missing-output treatment, ties, significance tests, multiplicity, margins and numerical thresholds before inference. The following are candidate gates, not permission to choose thresholds after results:

1. **`TRIANGULATION_EXPERIMENT_EFFICIENCY_SUPPORTED`:** materially lower restricted mean probes-to-identification than P and A; superiority tests over paired machines with multiplicity control; a substantial identification fraction; no loss concealed by censoring; and a corresponding actuator-cost benefit or an explicitly narrower probe-count claim. Compare B separately rather than claiming an enumeration advantage automatically.
2. **`TRIANGULATION_PREDICTIVE_UNDERSTANDING_SUPPORTED`:** the efficiency benefit accompanies prospectively defined non-inferior or better held-out prediction. Choose a confidence-based non-inferiority margin before inference; a nonsignificant difference is not evidence of equivalence.
3. **`SHORT_EXPERIMENT_TO_LONG_SEQUENCE_GENERALIZATION`:** correct unseen length-4/5/6 traces exceed the qualified trivial references and the registered paired comparator criteria. Specify absolute and relative performance floors before world selection.
4. **`TRIANGULATION_GOAL_DIRECTED_BENEFIT_SUPPORTED`:** target attainment improves under the identical fixed planner, budget, goal and tie rules, with a paired success test and a registered successful-action non-regression definition.

No positive gate may be declared from explanation quality, self-assessment, favorable anecdotes, an unregistered subgroup, or the mere production of a changed internal state. Unsupported component-level causal claims require separate ablations; this design does not silently treat descriptive Time/Relation/Direction metrics as randomized ablations.

## Qualification and integrity requirements

The preceding failure motivates stronger synthetic legal-output qualification. Register the new-study schema/transport mechanism prospectively and apply the same legality boundary to every model-selected arm. If grammar constraints are used, they may enforce syntax and membership in the public allowed catalogue only. They must not rank probes, impose shortest-first choices, consult hypotheses or use scientific outcomes. Distinguish such a runtime change from the earlier unconstrained runtime in reporting.

Qualify exact model identity, complete ledger fit, structured proposal output, legal catalogue membership, deterministic decoding and GPU stability using synthetic fixtures only. No scientific truncation, retries, semantic repair or silent fallback. Invalid-output handling must be chosen and frozen before the first scientific call. Do not reuse this study's scientific worlds as engineering fixtures.

Freeze method, hypotheses, worlds, seeds, prompts, interface, ledger/state update rules, budgets, schemas, planner, claim definitions, queries, scoring and gates. Then collect all authenticated raw evidence, commit its public hash manifest, and only afterward score model correctness, semantic claims and information metrics. Require byte-identical zero-inference replay and audits of actual rendered prompts, observation provenance, oracle isolation, sealed-query separation and prior-branch preservation.

Publish a completed negative or terminal failure as such. Preserve raw private outputs, model reasoning, signed streams, keys, binaries and private databases locally. Publish only an audited branch and public history. No merge, promotion, training or subsequent RSI experiment is included.
