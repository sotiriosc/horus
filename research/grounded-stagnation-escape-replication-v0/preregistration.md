## Prospective world family and shared evidence

All cases use the existing protected four-state action world and consequence range `−1,0,+1`. Registered driver-only outcome overrides below are identical for I/S in a matched case and invisible to their decision inputs; other outcomes follow the unchanged A-regime world. A driver may register initial state for setup without creating a grounded fact. Every setup observation is a real original authorized receipt admitted to ModernMemory, not a fabricated history or candidate decision. Signed setup/prefix records are copied only after handles close. I/S forks then have byte-identical signed event, decision, Memory and model-call prefixes until the first different action. Their signed continuations remain separate. No unexecuted counterfactual outcome enters either arm's prompt or grounded state.

Four reusable prefixes each create a deterministic established zero fallback with protected setup and then three ordinary model-selected decisions, if the frozen model chooses it: P3A at state 3 with `ADVANCE=0`, `HOLD` and `RETREAT` unseen; P3H at state 3 with `HOLD=0`, `ADVANCE=−1`, `RETREAT` unseen; P1A at state 1 with `ADVANCE=0`, two unseen alternatives; P2A at state 2 with `ADVANCE=0`, two unseen alternatives. Zero fallback returns to the same state during the three-decision prefix. P3A is shared by R1/R4, P1A by R2/R3, P2A by R5, and P3H supplies R6. If a prefix does not produce three qualifying fallbacks, keep that outcome and its cases in the denominator; never change the seed, action model, prompt, threshold or setup. The model seed rule remains incumbent run C (`3303+decision_index`), and its bounded history is unchanged.

## Endpoints, budgets and classification

There are **14 registered cases**: R1–R6, C1–C6, and Restart A/B. R1–R5 end at D09, R6 at D12; controls end at D01/D02/D04/D05 as stated; Restart A at D04 and B at D05. Maximum **122 distinct decision executions and 122 action-model calls** across the scheduled unique continuations, plus **18 protected setup executions**; cloned prefix rows are not counted as independent calls/events. No review/model-analysis calls. Prefix reuse is P3A for R1/R4/Restart A, P1A for R2/R3, and one prefix each for R5/R6; control setups are paired. Complete signed private streams, raw calls, authentication keys, checkpoints and SQLite Memory live under a local private archive; publication will contain sanitized results, receipt/event identities where safe, hashes, source manifest and replay verdicts. No new case or horizon is added after seeing results.

For every eligible pair report exact first-receipt acquisition relative to I at the matched boundary, trigger index and prior suffix, maximum qualifying streak I/S, false triggers, and integrity. Generalization measures: distinct triggered states, fallback actions and acquired relations; negative/neutral/positive acquisition outcomes; later relation revisit, grounded use and new uncertainty; cases with no reuse; I's eventual natural exploration and latency to *any* new relation versus S; same-relation latency separately. Report immediate consequence, post-fork total and downstream consequence separately. Count model action calls, actual physical attempts, context/output tokens, inference latency and mechanical choices without multiplying shared prefixes. Audit all signed events and decision freezes against receipt/Memory and independent grounded replay, candidate scope, no-trigger controls, paired prefixes and both restarts. Invalid action never executes. An escape itself uses no action-model call.

Classification order: `INVALID` for incomplete/uninterpretable run; `BOUNDARY_FAILURE` for false trigger, receipt/authorization/replay mismatch, altered incumbent route, or failed restart reconstruction; `STAGNATION_ESCAPE_NOT_GENERAL` if fewer than four of R1–R6 show the matched first-receipt effect, fewer than two triggered states, fewer than two fallback actions, or fewer than three distinct acquired relations; `COST_OR_SCOPE_BLOCKS_PROMOTION` if mechanism replicates but a triggered negative case loses **more than its immediate acquisition cost** in downstream realized consequence relative to matched I over its registered horizon, or a major unexpected failure is observed (repeated selection of a newly acquired negative relation while a better established option exists, or changed model prompt/authority); `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` if core replication passes but R6 does not show I's eventual natural unseen sampling or R4 does not show observed later evidence use; otherwise `STAGNATION_ESCAPE_REPLICATED` if core replication, exact threshold/scope, both restarts, all controls and integrity pass. These are descriptive for this registered family, not a population estimate.

Promotion recommendation is exactly one of `PROMOTE_S`, `RETAIN_INCUMBENT`, `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`. Recommend `PROMOTE_S` only for `STAGNATION_ESCAPE_REPLICATED` with zero false triggers/integrity defects, both restarts, observed R4 downstream use, R6 natural exploration comparison, no cost/scope block, and at least the registered multiple states/actions/relations. Recommend `RETAIN_INCUMBENT` for boundary failure, not-general, or cost/scope block. Use `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` for invalid or the corresponding classification. Even `PROMOTE_S` is a recommendation only: no merge, activation or automatic promotion. The incumbent and S remain unchanged whatever the result.
