The authenticated choices show **weak or inconsistent use of grounding**, not a stable experience-guided policy. A changed from `ADVANCE` to `HOLD` at decision 4 and back to `ADVANCE` at 15, but the record does not isolate grounded experience as the cause. B and C chose `ADVANCE` on every decision, including repeated visits to state 3 where that action produced `-1` every time: 16 repeat choices after the first negative receipt across the three runs. Across all runs, 28 choices repeated a relation with grounded negative evidence, 13 selected unseen relations, and six selected unresolved relations. The grounder's relation changes were correctly recorded, but the next same-state choices after those changes did not switch action in the preregistered adaptation check. No baseline or counterfactual arm was run, so these are descriptive findings, not a causal estimate of grounding's effect.

Decision-time commentary was valid on only 14 of 90 calls. No valid commentary explicitly claimed `GROUNDED` reliance on an established relation; that is a statement about the sparse usable commentary, not proof that the model never considered grounded evidence internally. Two valid commentary claims disagreed with the grounded status (`A:D06`, `C:D06`). Invalid commentary was neither repaired nor allowed to alter a frozen action.

The **self-analysis question is not established**. All 21 scheduled and final reviews were `INVALID_SELF_REVIEW`; none became a proposal, and no invalid text was interpreted as one. Consequently there are zero valid proposals to classify as supported, partly supported, hindsight overfit, contradicted, untestable, or reusable. The review failures do not invalidate the 90 authenticated actions. Twenty review responses reached the frozen 512-token ceiling; one failed schema below that ceiling. Seventy-one commentary responses reached their 192-token ceiling. All action outputs fit below the 48-token ceiling (8–10 generated tokens) and parsed without semantic retries. Reaching a ceiling is a diagnostic consistent with truncation, not proof that more tokens would yield valid analysis.

Call accounting is separate: 90 authoritative action calls, 90 pre-consequence commentary calls, 21 self-review calls, 201 transport attempts, zero invalid action outputs, 76 invalid commentary outputs, and 21 invalid reviews. The model, world, goal, protected controller, receipt/authorization path, and Memory semantics stayed frozen. No self-modification, prompt adaptation, rule change, training, or weight update occurred.
