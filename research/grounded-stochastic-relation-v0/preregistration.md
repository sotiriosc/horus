# Grounded stochastic relation v0 — prospective registration

Parent safe-fallback study is frozen at `cb1a451b441c81ffe63c16f74d1089c73e84f6d3` (`SAFE_FALLBACK_SUPPORTED`). No parent evidence is modified. No training, planner, Horus or Zakhor additions, or hybrid-controller change.

## Representation

D imports the unchanged `grounded_uncertainty_state_v0.uncertainty.fold`. E folds only exact-relation admitted authenticated ModernMemory receipt rows, after reconciliation with the signed protected event stream. E retains count, all outcome counts, all chronological outcomes, every event identity/receipt hash/stream sequence, a four-receipt recent window, current segment counts and observed count/denominator frequencies. There are no hidden probabilities or learned parameters. Tie in modal point resolves to first-seen value. E's point is the modal value in its current segment, or identity-state/zero if unseen; D uses its frozen point rule. Points are secondary.

After at least six outcomes in the current segment before a four-receipt comparison window, E says `POSSIBLE_REGIME_CHANGE` if the four most recent outcomes are identical and differ from the mode of all earlier outcomes in that segment. This requires four surprising receipts. On the next identical receipt, E confirms a new segment starting at the first of the four, retaining the old history and its exact receipt support. A different fifth receipt cancels the warning. Otherwise E says `EMPIRICALLY_STABLE` if its current segment contains one observed outcome, `VARIABLE_RELATION` if it contains more, or `UNSEEN`. These are empirical statements, never claims that unobserved outcomes are impossible. A possible warning is not a claim of a proven shift.

## Workloads and scoring

`protocol.py` freezes exact observed consequences, phases, actions, model checkpoint indices, the G restart point, the window and minimum prior. All outcomes are injected at the external world execution boundary before original protected receipts are issued; each D/E/M session independently publishes matched observations via the unchanged authorization and ModernMemory paths. The schedule is hidden from the folds and model requests. M runs frozen joint Map + G2 only at prospectively fixed checkpoints; neutral forecasts on other observations maintain its matched receipt history. M never edits state.

A deterministic, B stationary variable, C isolated anomaly, D persistent shift, E shift then restoration, F matched ten-receipt prefix with divergent stationary/shift futures, and G two exact relations with fresh-process restart at one variable and one possible-change state. `F` prefix requires `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT_TO_DISTINGUISH`; identical receipt values cannot disclose their future. G uses transit actions to reach state 2 and return. One noncurrent unauthorized Memory admission after restart must fail without entering history.

Primary metrics are possible-change false alarms in A/B/C/F-stationary and the stationary portions of other schedules, missed persistent shifts after the fifth new-mode receipt, warning latency, isolated-anomaly tolerance, restoration warning/confirmation, fraction of variable/possible states, false claims of certainty, exact fold-vs-observable-history consistency, and restart equality of authenticated files and reconstructed states/receipt support. Secondary metrics are point accuracy (M only on probe events), storage bytes, fold seconds, model calls, context tokens, and serialized E bytes. Both D unresolved alerts and E possible alerts are tabulated, while D's `UNRESOLVED_CHANGE` is interpreted as unresolved rather than a confirmed change. Truth phases are used only by the analyzer.

Classification: `EMPIRICAL_GROUNDING_SUPPORTED` if no E false alarms on registered stationary histories, all persistent shifts/restoration produce warnings by the fourth new-mode receipt and confirmations by the fifth, no isolated anomaly warns, F prefix remains unresolved, and restart/perturbation/history audits pass. `DETERMINISTIC_STATE_SUFFICIENT` if D attains the same distinctions without excess unresolved warnings. `CHANGE_VARIABILITY_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` if registered histories remain indistinguishable after the available post-prefix evidence. `MIXED` if only some classes meet these criteria. Any integrity failure is `INVALID`. The study is a bounded demonstration of this frozen rule, not universal stochastic change detection.

Existing H_SAFE's deterministic threshold can only mean guaranteed nonnegative when authenticated consequences support an invariant; observed mostly-nonnegative empirical frequencies cannot justify that word. No H_SAFE behavior will be changed.
