# Phase 3 readiness: stop after diagnosis

Phase 1 mapped the active [components](architecture-map.md), [authority](authority-map.md), and [uncertainty](uncertainty-map.md). Phase 2 checked the [campaign sequence](campaign-diagnosis.md), [Run C receipts](run-c-trace.md), [recurring costs](recurring-inefficiencies.md), and [falsifiable hypotheses](hypotheses.md). The executable action, world, authorization, Memory, grounded folds, model, prompts, and recovery rules were not changed. This milestone made **zero local model calls and zero world executions**.

The evidence supports a narrow diagnosis: established deterministic ceiling facts can be given mechanical authority, while the current mixed route permits a model-selected known-zero fallback indefinitely. It does not tell us what either unseen Run C action would have produced. A valid empirical relation is also not a deterministic safety guarantee. The completed self-diagnosis attempt remains `INVALID` from transport timeouts, so no claim that the current local model can diagnose itself follows.

For a future separately authorized Phase 3, the open choices are *which single bounded hypothesis to test*, what effect to predict prospectively, what matched incumbent condition to preserve, and how to audit realized consequences and integrity. The present documents choose none of those rules or thresholds. A Phase 4 comparison would require preregistration, matched conditions, authenticated receipts, and a rollback criterion; it is not begun here.

Today, exploration and diagnosis are an **external meta-process** performed by Codex and the research harness over sanitized, authenticated records. A future agent self-process could inspect its own architecture and trajectory and formulate non-authoritative hypotheses, with proposals evaluated outside the agent before any promotion. Neither a valid self-proposal nor a promotion mechanism is demonstrated or authorized here. Grounded experience remains authoritative evidence; decision reasoning and retrospective analysis remain interpretations; a proposed improvement remains a hypothesis.

The completed scientific records retain their classifications: v0 pilot and v0.1 `INVALID`, v0.2 behaviorally `VALID`, grounded-authority `GROUNDED_AUTHORITY_SUPPORTED`, and self-diagnosis `INVALID`. No historical lineage is rewritten. The publication branch is a secret-free descendant of the prior public research lineage; complete private evidence remains in local archival commits outside its ancestry, as documented by each campaign's publication manifest.
