# Recurring inefficiencies and failure classes (Phase 2)

These are observed costs or structural risks, not approved remedies. See the [campaign table](campaign-diagnosis.md) for validity and denominator limits.

| Pattern | Supporting evidence | Boundary / failure class | Limit of inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model calls for questions already settled by exact facts | [Hybrid](../grounded-hybrid-controller-v0/report.md) used 12 model calls vs 46 model-only; [current authority](../grounded-authority-autonomous-agent-v0/result.md) mechanically selected 28 ceiling actions, avoiding those action calls. | Mechanical authority reduces inference cost and guards known-fact comparison; earlier model-only sensitivity was a bounded `DECISION_FAILURE`. | The call counts belong to different registered conditions; they do not measure a universal speedup. |
| Repeated actions without new relation knowledge | [Run C](run-c-trace.md) repeated HOLD zero 26 times after first discovery; ADVANCE and RETREAT at state 0 remained unseen. | `DECISION_FAILURE` in mixed candidate choice plus persistent `KNOWLEDGE_FAILURE` about alternatives. | New receipts did increase HOLD support and provenance; “no new information” means no newly sampled relation or changed value/status. |
| Uncertainty requires revisiting its exact relation | [Uncertainty study](../grounded-uncertainty-state-v0/report.md) resolved registered contradictions on later receipts; [stochastic study](../grounded-stochastic-relation-v0/report.md) confirmed warnings on later challenger receipts. | Authenticity/evidence boundary: no passive resolution. `KNOWLEDGE_FAILURE` if a relation is never resampled. | These studies demonstrate resolution under their fixed schedules, not a measured rate of abandoned unresolved relations in the current campaign. |
| Safe fallback can become a fixed point | The historical [safe-fallback study](../grounded-safe-fallback-v0/report.md) took six extra justified zero actions without higher reward; current [Run C](run-c-trace.md) has 26 zero fallbacks despite unseen alternatives. | Action justified by a nonnegative deterministic receipt is weaker than optimality established. Current loop is `DECISION_FAILURE` under the model's bounded authority. | The policies differ: historical H_SAFE was mechanical; current fallback is model selected. The two campaigns do not prove a common cause. |
| Grounding may be present yet behaviorally inert | [V0.2](../grounded-autonomous-agent-v0.2/result.md) made repeated negative-grounded choices; [sensitivity](../grounded-action-sensitivity-v0/report.md) found 0/9 paired evidence swaps changed action; [current authority](../grounded-authority-autonomous-agent-v0/result.md) eliminated the narrow known-negative-with-better-established error by route. | `DECISION_FAILURE` for tested model-only comparisons; mechanical route protects known exact facts. | Sensitivity is causal for those fixed contexts only; later Run C does not show the model universally ignores evidence. |
| Retrospective analysis has high interface cost and low usable yield | [V0.2](../grounded-autonomous-agent-v0.2/result.md): 0/21 valid reviews; [current authority](../grounded-authority-autonomous-agent-v0/result.md): 3/21 valid, all `NO_CHANGE_PROPOSED`, 2,824s review latency; [self-diagnosis](../grounded-self-diagnosis-v0/result.md): no responses after transport timeouts. | `INTERFACE/OPERATIONS`, not an integrity or demonstrated reasoning failure. Reviews had no authority over decisions. | Invalid/absent reviews cannot be scored as wrong behavioral proposals. |
| Full provenance increases durable state | [Stochastic report](../grounded-stochastic-relation-v0/report.md) measured a 7,953-byte largest serialized empirical state; [ModernMemory](../../experiments/modern_memory_vs_horus_v0/storage.py) retains chronological identities. | Integrity cost: audit and replay need provenance. | Bounded campaign footprint, not an asymptotic benchmark or a warrant to discard evidence. |

No inspected valid campaign establishes a failure of the receipt/authorization/Memory integrity path. Where outcomes were not executed, epistemic ignorance remains; where available grounded values failed to affect fixed-context choices, the limitation is in decision use. Those categories must not be conflated with the v0/v0.1 or retrospective `INVALID` operational outcomes.
