# Architecture boundary and observational method

This Phase 1/2 diagnosis is a read-only projection of previously published, replay-audited evidence. Its parent is secret-free R128 publication commit `40dbe69673e9b146888fdbb08c3df713aef6f336`. The R128 [public decision export](../qwen3-r128-autonomous-grounded-agent-v0/public-result.json), [substitution export](../qwen3-grounded-agent-substitution-v0/stage-b-public.json), [stochastic schedules](../grounded-stochastic-relation-v0/evidence/schedules), and frozen source are the inputs. `analyze.py` reads JSON and writes derived JSON; it has no model, simulator, receipt-issuer, SessionStore, or Memory interface. Authenticated provenance was verified in the source campaigns; this study does not re-authenticate private signed streams.

The active incumbent is grounded authority plus the **unchanged** threshold-3 `BOUNDED_STAGNATION_ESCAPE`, implemented in `grounded_agent` and frozen `experiments/grounded_stagnation_escape_evaluation_v0/candidate.py`. The latter counts signed, receipt-matched repetitions of the *same state and action* only where the chosen relation is **deterministic `ESTABLISHED` consequence exactly 0**, the action is a safe fallback and a self-loop, an alternative is `UNSEEN`, and no deterministic established +1 exists. The suffix is reconstructed from authenticated history. After three qualifying completed decisions, one canonical unseen action may be selected without an action-model call. State, fallback identity, nonqualifying choice, and the escape break/reset that suffix. The established +1 ceiling takes precedence. Nothing in this study edits or broadens these conditions.

R128 instead chose empirical `1:HOLD` 30/30 times per run. The first receipt made it `EMPIRICALLY_STABLE`; a 0 receipt at D03 made it `VARIABLE_RELATION`. It never became deterministic established 0; consequences included +1, 0, and -1. `1:ADVANCE` and `1:RETREAT` remained `UNSEEN` with zero observations. All 90 S eligibility flags are false for the structural relation-type requirement. The relation's realized consequence totals +4 per run; that does not erase the missing relation evidence.

The two mechanisms share a research motivation—bounded acquisition of missing relation evidence after repeated sampling—but have different evidence semantics. S's deterministic exact-zero signed suffix provides a guaranteed neutral fallback premise. An empirical relation has frequencies, recency, and possible regime changes; no finite sample makes its next outcome guaranteed. Any empirical-information candidate therefore needs a separately preregistered evaluation, not an S code extension inferred from analogy.

`MISSING_RELATION_EVIDENCE` means that a currently allowed relation has zero authenticated observations. A first selection of that action would necessarily add at least one authenticated relation observation **if execution and admission succeed**. It does not imply that the action is good or safe in all applications. Candidate predicates in the accompanying analyzer read only pre-decision grounded assessments and authenticated prior consequences. Registered schedule phase labels are applied only after candidate calculation. Outcomes for unexecuted alternatives are not read, estimated, or used to choose thresholds.

The three R128 runs have independent signed source, receipt, and Memory identities, while their 30-action, 30-consequence, and grounded-kind semantic traces match exactly. They establish reproducibility of the observed behavior under this registered family, not three independent patterns or an unseen-action counterfactual.
