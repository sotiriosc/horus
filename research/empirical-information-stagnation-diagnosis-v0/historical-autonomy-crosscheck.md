# Historical autonomous comparison

The [v0.2 Dolphin public runs](../grounded-autonomous-agent-v0.2/public-result.json) and [grounded-authority Dolphin public runs](../grounded-authority-autonomous-agent-v0/public-result.json) contain no *selected observed empirical* relation: the selected grounded relations in those exports are deterministic `UNSEEN`, `ESTABLISHED`, or `UNRESOLVED_CHANGE`. They cannot prove that Dolphin naturally left an already observed empirical relation to sample an unseen alternative. Some Dolphin runs explored deterministic unseen relations; that is a different decision context.

The directly matched [D/Q-N substitution runs](../qwen3-grounded-agent-substitution-v0/stage-b-public.json) sharpen that distinction. D1 and D2 selected unseen deterministic `ADVANCE` at D01 from state 1, before observing `1:HOLD`, then traversed states 2, 3, and 0 and acquired four distinct deterministic relations in each run. D3 selected unseen empirical `HOLD` at D01, then continued HOLD through D30 after `1:HOLD` became observed; it never sampled the two unseen state-1 alternatives. Thus Dolphin shows early *initial-action* variation, but the historical D3 path is an empirical-stagnation counterexample to any claim that Dolphin necessarily exits it. D1/D2 plus D3 yielded the previously reported nine newly observed relations, including D3's empirical HOLD. The D1/D2 and D3 environments and selected paths differ, so this is descriptive, not a controlled causal comparison.

Q-N runs Q1/Q2/Q3 each selected HOLD 30/30 times from state 1, with `1:HOLD` empirical after D01 and `ADVANCE`/`RETREAT` unseen throughout. The [R128 public runs](../qwen3-r128-autonomous-grounded-agent-v0/public-result.json) T1/T2/T3 repeat that semantic trajectory under the qualified thinking interface. In both Q-N and R128, every post-D01 choice remained on an observed empirical relation; R128's native bounded reasoning did not change that. D3 provides the same structural failure with Dolphin on this schedule. Therefore this study diagnoses a policy/context gap exposed by the agent sequence, not a uniquely Qwen failure or a demonstrated Dolphin remedy.

Immediately before D1/D2 exploration, `1:ADVANCE` was `UNSEEN` and no observed empirical `1:HOLD` competed. Immediately before Q-N/R128 repetition and D3 repetition, HOLD was empirical and the deterministic alternatives were `UNSEEN`. The crucial observed difference is **which relation was sampled first**, not evidence of a later empirical-to-unseen switch. No observed autonomous run here supplies a valid experimental estimate of the benefit or cost of such a switch.
