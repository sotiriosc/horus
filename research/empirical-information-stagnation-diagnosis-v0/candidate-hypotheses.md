# Falsifiable Phase 3 candidate hypotheses

These are **diagnostic predicates**, not policy code. `analyze.py` calculates each from the pre-decision prefix only. The shared scope is: selected relation is empirical `EMPIRICALLY_STABLE` or `VARIABLE_RELATION`; at least one currently allowed *other* relation is truly `UNSEEN`; and no deterministic established +1 action exists. This scope prevents a missing-evidence argument from overriding the grounded +1 ceiling. All counts below are *would-signal decision contexts under unchanged observed history*, not simulated policy choices. If a future intervention acquired an unseen relation, its subsequent eligibility would need new authenticated history; it cannot be inferred by continuing the unchanged HOLD trace.

The four-observation span reuses the **already frozen empirical window length**, not a threshold optimized for R128 reward. H2's cutoffs are the natural nonpositive sign boundary for both existing evidence summaries. Neither sign estimates the unobserved action's return. H1's “no material novelty” is narrow: counts, denominators, recency, and receipt provenance still change at every observation. Its first four qualifying prior updates are D07–D10, so it first appears before D11. H3's first four variable pre-assessments are D04–D07, so it first appears before D08. H4 first appears before D05 once D01–D04 have completed.

All four would repeat on later unchanged R128 decisions: H1 at D11–D30; H2 at D19–D23; H3 at D08–D30; H4 at D05–D30. That is a reason a future mechanism must be **one-shot per missing-relation acquisition**, not permission for repeated forced exploration. Signal counts are 3 times these per-run counts only because the three semantic traces match; the independent receipts do not create independent structural patterns.

The stochastic [cross-check JSON](empirical-crosscheck.json) uses the frozen **E arm's pre-observation** states. A/B/C/F-stationary are useful benign controls; D/E/F-shift show behavior under deterioration and restoration. The schedules record one empirical relation's observations, not a contemporaneous set of alternative-action assessments. Their proxy calculation conditionally assumes an unseen alternative and no established +1 **only to test the empirical signal**. A hit is not a proven false policy trigger; absence is likewise limited. H1/H3/H4 have many benign proxy hits and cannot by themselves distinguish R128 stagnation from ordinary variability. H2 has no hits in the tested stationary/anomaly proxies and does appear during persistent shifts. In E_SHIFT_RESTORE it signals at D15 in shift and D16–D17 at the start of restoration, then ceases as positive receipts accrue; the exact rows and phase labels are in the JSON. G_RESTART has no H2 proxy hits, and its relation/state transitions make it inappropriate to pool into a single uninterrupted suffix.

Falsifiers for **H1**: prospective benign-variable contexts with unseen alternatives that satisfy the no-novelty span but should keep sampling the empirical action; B is already a warning. For **H2**: a prospective benign stationary context satisfying both nonpositive signs plus true missing alternatives, or an authenticated paired test in which the one-shot evidence acquisition causes material harm; conversely, repeated empirically positive fixation would expose low sensitivity. For **H3**: benign variable relations with uncovered but irrelevant alternatives; B/C already show the signal is broad. For **H4**: strongly positive empirical contexts with low-value or dangerous unseen alternatives; A/B/C demonstrate its lack of a deterioration guard. None may be validated using historical outcomes of an unexecuted action.

`SELECTED_PHASE3_HYPOTHESIS: H2 — empirical deterioration + missing relation evidence`, strictly as a candidate for separately preregistered evaluation. H2 alone meets the requested conditional stationary discrimination in this preserved set, uses only authenticated pre-decision summaries, and leaves reward direction of missing actions unknown. Phase 3 must test the *complete* action-context predicate, one-shot bound, receipt path, restart, and costs before any policy promotion. No threshold or action rule is activated here.
